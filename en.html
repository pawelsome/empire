    A conversation about goals, a look at existing systems and constraints. No commitment.

  2. Scope and quote

    A staged plan, timeline and cost. You know what you pay for before we start.

  3. Delivery

    Short iterations, with a working version to review after each one.

  4. Maintenance

    Monitoring, updates, security patches and further development.
